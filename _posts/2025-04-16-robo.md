I've wanted something from this artist for years now, so was super excited when I finally noticed he had his books open. I proposed Robo, from Chrono Trigger because I love the story behind him. A robot that was repaired and is eager to help with anything, and wants to make the world better. As you progress, you can make a trip to the future, where he was made, and he finds out his original programming was actually to destroy the last of humanity. He decides that who he used to be, or was intended to be, doesn't matter compared to what he **chooses** to be. 

Artist: [Mambo Tattooer](https://www.instagram.com/mambotattooer) working out of his own studio in Milan, Italy.
